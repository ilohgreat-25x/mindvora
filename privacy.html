Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to Mindvora. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your information when you use the Mindvora app and website.

By using Mindvora, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. 👤 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Mindvora:

Information you provide directly:

  • Full name and username (handle)
  • Email address and password
  • Profile photo and biography
  • Posts, comments, messages and stories you create
  • Phone number (only when purchasing airtime or data)
  • Bank account details (only when requesting a withdrawal)
  • Payment information processed securely through Paystack

Information collected automatically:

  • Device type, operating system and browser
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages and features you interact with
  • Time and duration of your sessions
  • Crash reports and error logs

2. 🎯 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Mindvora account
  • Deliver and improve the Mindvora app and its features
  • Process payments, airtime top-ups and withdrawals
  • Send notifications about activity on your account
  • Detect and prevent fraud, scams and security threats
  • Respond to your support requests and enquiries
  • Personalise your feed and content recommendations
  • Send important account and security updates
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. 🔗 Information We Share

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use trusted third-party services to operate Mindvora, including Firebase (Google) for authentication and database, Cloudinary for media storage, Paystack for payments, EmailJS for emails, and NOWPayments for crypto transactions. These providers only receive the data necessary to perform their services.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order or government authority.
  • Safety and security: We may share information to protect the rights, safety and property of Mindvora users or the public.
  • With your consent: We may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

4. 🔒 How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of your data seriously. Mindvora uses industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Firebase Security Rules to protect your Firestore data
  • OTP email verification for new accounts
  • Automatic detection and banning of suspicious accounts
  • Rate limiting to prevent brute force attacks
  • Input sanitisation to prevent injection attacks
  • Secure payment processing — we never store your card details

⚠️ While we do everything we can to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable all security features available in the app.

5. 🍪 Cookies and Tracking

Mindvora uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and improve your experience. These include:

  • Session cookies to maintain your login state
  • Preference cookies to remember your settings such as language and theme
  • Analytics data to understand how the app is used

You can clear cookies at any time through your browser or device settings. Note that clearing cookies may log you out of Mindvora.

6. 👶 Children's Privacy

Mindvora is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account on Mindvora, please contact us immediately at mindvoraofficial@outlook.com and we will delete the account promptly.

7. 🌍 Data Storage and Transfer

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers which may be located outside your country of residence. By using Mindvora, you consent to the transfer of your data to these servers. We ensure that all our service providers maintain adequate data protection standards.

8. ⏳ How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. Specifically:

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account
  • Posts and content are kept until you delete them
  • Payment records are kept for 7 years for legal compliance
  • Security logs are kept for 90 days
  • Deleted account data is permanently removed within 30 days

9. ✅ Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can view your profile and account data at any time inside the app.
  • Correction: You can edit your profile information at any time from your profile settings.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account from the app settings. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Data portability: You can request a copy of your data by emailing us.
  • Opt out of notifications: You can turn off push notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: You can withdraw consent to any optional data processing by contacting us.

10. 💳 Payments and Financial Data

All payments on Mindvora are processed by Paystack and NOWPayments — both PCI DSS compliant payment processors. Mindvora never stores your card number, bank account PIN or any sensitive financial credentials.

Withdrawal requests are processed manually within 24 to 48 hours. We verify all withdrawal requests before processing to prevent fraud.

11. 🔔 Push Notifications

With your permission, Mindvora may send you push notifications about new messages, likes, comments, followers and security alerts. You can disable push notifications at any time in your device settings or within the Mindvora app.

12. 🔗 Third-Party Links

Mindvora may contain links to external websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any external sites you visit from within Mindvora.

13. 📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. The date at the top of this policy shows when it was last updated.

Your continued use of Mindvora after any changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.

14. ⚖️ Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any disputes relating to this policy will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of Nigerian courts.
